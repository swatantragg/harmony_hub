01 — Positioning

The modern studio, in daylight

Maestro is where a music company keeps every master, cover, video, reel and lyric for a release — searchable, versioned, and always accounted for. The Studio direction makes that feel effortless: a clean white canvas, a confident indigo, and a warm amber spark for the one action that matters most on any screen.

Clear
Generous whitespace and plain labels. You always know where you are and what a button will do.
Fast
Crisp type, tight components, instant feedback. The interface feels as quick as the uploads.
Dependable
Calm status colours and honest states. The system tells you the truth about every file.
Warm
A single amber accent keeps a technical tool feeling human and made for creative people.

02 — Logo & wordmark

The mark

A five-bar equaliser in a soft, rounded tile — with the tallest bar struck in amber as the downbeat. The wordmark is set in Space Grotesk: geometric, current, and unmistakably a product.

Maestro

Clear space & sizing

Keep clear space equal to the tile height on every side. Minimum tile size is 24 px; below that, use the tile alone. The amber bar is always the centre (tallest) bar — never move it.

Maestro

Usage rules

  • ✓ Indigo bars, one amber downbeat, on a white tile.
  • ✓ On indigo backgrounds, flip the tile to white.
  • ✕ Never make all bars amber or all indigo.
  • ✕ Never add a gradient or shadow to the bars.
  • ✕ Never set the wordmark in the body font (Jakarta).

Three voices, clear roles
